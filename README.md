# English
# 🧹 FastNotificationRemove

A small **quality-of-life addon** for **World of Warcraft** that automatically removes annoying **NEW badges** and **notification markers** from Blizzard's settings interface.

After patches or expansions Blizzard often marks many options as **NEW**, even if they are not relevant to most players.  
This addon keeps your settings **clean and uncluttered**.

---

## ✨ Features

✔ Removes **NEW badges** from Blizzard settings  
✔ Removes **Micro Menu notifications**  
✔ Works automatically on login  
✔ Optional debug mode  
✔ Lightweight and performance friendly  
✔ Multi-language support

---

## ⚙️ Options

The addon includes a small configuration panel inside the **Blizzard Addon Settings**.

**Settings:**

- Remove **Settings NEW badges**
- Remove **Micro Menu notifications**
- Enable **Debug mode**

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Blizzard often marks options with **NEW** even after you've already seen them.

This addon automatically:

- marks them as **seen**
- removes **notification markers**
- keeps your UI **clean and tidy**

---

## 🌍 Language Support

The addon supports multiple WoW client languages, including:

- English
- German
- French
- Spanish
- Italian
- Portuguese
- Russian
- Korean
- Chinese

---

## 📦 Compatibility

✔ WoW Retail  
✔ Midnight UI  
✔ Works standalone (no dependencies required)
